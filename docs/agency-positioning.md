## Features That Already Sell to Agencies (Built)
- **Tester email list** — one-click copy of all approved tester emails, ready to paste into Play Console. Agencies don't manually hunt for emails across application cards.
- **Daily Missions** — 14 structured daily tasks testers complete. Agencies can show clients a paper trail of engagement, not just "12 people signed up."
- **Mission Tracker grid** — 14-column completion grid per job. Agencies can screenshot this and send it to clients as a compliance report.
- **Per-job isolation** — each client app has its own tester pool, email list, and mission set. No crossover between client jobs.
- **Testing Report** — exportable report per job for agency client handoff.
